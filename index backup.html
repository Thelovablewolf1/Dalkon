Common questions

Questions we get asked before a project starts.

If yours isn't answered here, send it through — we'll give you a straight answer without the sales pitch.

How much does a website cost in Zimbabwe?

Cost depends on scope. A focused one-page or landing site sits at the lower end, a multi-page corporate website with a content management system sits in the middle, and e-commerce or custom systems are quoted individually.

We give a written fixed quote before any work begins, so there are no surprises halfway through. Send us your requirements and we'll come back with a breakdown.

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page typically takes one to two weeks. A standard corporate website takes three to five weeks. E-commerce stores and custom business systems usually run six weeks or more depending on features.

Timelines depend heavily on how quickly content, images and feedback come back from your side — we'll flag that up front so the schedule is realistic.

Do you work with businesses outside Harare?

Yes. We're based in Harare and work with clients across Zimbabwe as well as internationally. Projects run remotely through video calls, email and WhatsApp, with in-person meetings available for clients in and around Harare.

Do you handle domain registration and hosting?

Yes. We can register your domain, set up hosting and configure business email on your own domain. You keep ownership of the domain and hosting account throughout, so you're never locked in to us.

Can you redesign our existing website?

Yes — redesigns are a common request. We review your current site for design, speed, mobile usability and search visibility, then rebuild it while keeping the pages and content that already perform well so you don't lose existing search rankings.

Do you build custom business software and internal tools?

Yes. Alongside websites we build custom business applications, customer portals and workflow automation tools. That includes replacing manual spreadsheet processes, digitising paper forms and automating repetitive admin work.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site we build ships with the technical search foundations in place: clean page structure, fast loading, mobile-first layouts, descriptive titles and meta descriptions, structured data, and a sitemap submitted to Google Search Console.

Ranking beyond that depends on ongoing content and local listings — we'll advise on what to do next, or manage it for you.

Do you sign confidentiality agreements?

Yes. Confidentiality is one of our core commitments. Client information is used only for the purpose it was shared, and never disclosed to third parties without explicit consent. We're happy to sign your NDA or provide ours.
